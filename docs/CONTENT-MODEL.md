# African Atlas — Map Content Model

Every public investigation is a **Map**, not a blog post or newsletter issue.

## Map anatomy

1. **Question** — one important question worth understanding.
2. **System boundary** — what is inside and outside the investigation.
3. **Actors** — people, organizations, institutions, communities.
4. **Flows** — money, goods, information, energy, trust, influence.
5. **Relationships** — what depends on what.
6. **Friction** — bottlenecks, failures, missing infrastructure.
7. **Feedback loops** — reinforcing or balancing dynamics.
8. **Leverage points** — where intervention could change the system.
9. **Future possibilities** — plausible pathways, not unsupported prediction.
10. **Connections** — links to other Maps in the Atlas.

## Initial map ideas

- Map 001 — Why don't more African businesses scale?
- Map 002 — How does food actually move?
- Map 003 — The economics of trust.
- Map 004 — What makes a corridor become an economy?
- Map 005 — How does capital move from ambition to infrastructure?
- Map 006 — What does technology amplify — and what can it never replace?

## Editorial principle

We do not chase headlines. We use events as entry points into deeper systems.

The goal is not to tell readers what to think. The goal is to give them a better map.
