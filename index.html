00 / PREMISE

We have spent too long looking at Africa in fragments.

Agriculture separated from logistics. Technology separated from people. Capital separated from infrastructure. Health separated from food. Policy separated from lived reality.

But nothing exists in isolation. The unit of understanding is not the sector. It is the relationship.

01 / SYSTEMS

One continent.
Many living systems.

Choose a system. Watch what it touches.

SYSTEM 00

Everything is connected.

The Atlas begins by refusing the false separation between sectors. Every system creates pressure, possibility and feedback in every other system.
